Proposed settlement: For the two-fighter pool, a technical decision pays the official winner and a technical draw refunds both sides. In the separate method pool, group technical decisions with decision and technical submissions with submission; refund the method pool on a draw or disqualification. No contest, cancellation, fighter replacement, or an unbacked winning selection → full market refund, no fee.
